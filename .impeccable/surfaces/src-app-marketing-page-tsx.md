## Direction contract

THESIS: Understanding arrives as text locked to the moment of speech — a caption, not a chat bubble or a quiz card. Refuses the generic gamified-quiz-app arrangement every language app in this category ships.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black cinematic ground (never pure black), letterboxed like a paused film frame. High-contrast bottom-anchored caption bars carry all primary content, two-line discipline throughout. Hebrew (RTL) and English (LTR) run as two stacked caption tracks — never merged, always distinguishable by weight/position, the way real bilingual subtitles run one language above another. One warm amber accent marks the "active" word/moment, the way a subtitle highlights a translated idiom. Raised from the orienteering challenger: a fixed base "map" layer (the learning path) with the active route drawn over it. Raised from Studio Dumbar: full commitment to saturated color blocking outside caption contexts, so chrome never goes fully monochrome. Raised from the phosphor-terminal challenger: the AI conversation surfaces read as a live typed transcript, not generic chat-bubble chrome.

STORY: A first-time visitor understands instantly — what you say gets understood, live, by something paying real attention. A learner acts, speaks or answers, watches their own words caption into being, watches the corrected form caption in beneath it in the same grammar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A dark cinematic hero, letterboxed, no video required — a real spoken English sentence live-captions across the frame, a Hebrew correction captions in beneath it a beat later, a timecode ticks. The primary action sits where a video player's next control would sit.
